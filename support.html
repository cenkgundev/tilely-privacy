Tilely Support / Destek

3D step-by-step building guides for standard 7.5 cm (3-inch) magnetic building tiles.

Which magnetic tiles work with Tilely?

Tilely works with all standard 7.5 cm (3-inch) magnetic building tile sets across 24 real tile shapes including squares, triangles, arches, windows, fences, car bases, ramps, and ball-run tubes.

How does "My Box" piece matching work?

Open the My Box tab, add a starter preset or adjust individual tile counts, and use the Build Now filter on Discover to see models your exact tiles can complete today—including smart substitutions when you are only missing a specialty tile.

1:1 AR Tabletop Preview

In any 3D build guide, tap the AR button to preview the finished model at true 1:1 scale on your table or rug. Make sure Camera permission is enabled in iOS Settings → Tilely → Camera.

Restore a purchase or manage subscription

  • Restore: Open Tilely Pro or Settings and tap Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account you purchased with.
  • Cancel / Manage: Open iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Tilely.
