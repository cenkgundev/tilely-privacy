Tilely Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Bundle ID: com.cenkgun.tilely

Tilely is an independent 3D magnetic tile building companion for iPhone and iPad designed for families, parents, and children with a strict privacy-by-design, 100% on-device architecture. The Tilely app does not collect, store on external servers, sell, rent, or share any personal data. We receive personal data only if you choose to contact us by email (section 6).

This policy serves as the information notice required by Article 13 of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Article 10 of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and U.S. state privacy laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Who we are

The data controller (under Turkish law, "veri sorumlusu") is the developer of Tilely, an individual developer based in Türkiye, shown as the seller on the App Store ("we", "us").

Contact: support@melyro.app

Tilely is an independent app designed around standard 7.5 cm (3-inch) magnetic building tile geometry. Tilely is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any physical magnetic tile, building block, or toy manufacturer.

2. Summary: Zero data collection

  • App Store Privacy Label: "Data Not Collected" and "No Tracking".
  • No accounts or login: No registration, email address, or profile is required to use Tilely.
  • No third-party SDKs: Tilely includes zero third-party analytics, crash-reporting trackers, advertising networks, or behavioral profiling SDKs.
  • No tracking: Tilely does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and never requests App Tracking Transparency permission.

3. Data stored exclusively on your device

To provide core app features offline, Tilely stores the following data strictly on your iPhone or iPad (using Apple SwiftData and UserDefaults). We have no access to it:

  • Tile Inventory ("My Box"): Piece counts and starter set presets you enter.
  • Build Journal: Optional completed build logs, notes, and real-world creation photos you choose to save.
  • App Preferences: Language, Light/Dark appearance, tile color palette, sound/haptics toggles, and studio preferences.
  • Subscription Entitlement Cache: A local cache of your Apple StoreKit 2 entitlement state so Tilely Pro stays responsive offline.

You can delete individual journal entries, reset your inventory, clear the thumbnail cache inside Settings, or delete all local data immediately by uninstalling Tilely.

4. Camera & Photo Picker access

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Requested only when you open the optional 1:1 Tabletop AR Preview feature (Apple ARKit & RealityKit). Live camera frames are processed strictly on-device in real time to detect flat surfaces and anchor 3D previews. Tilely never records, saves, or uploads camera video or images.
  • Photos (System PhotosPicker): When you add a finished build photo to your Build Journal or select a photo for on-device tile counting (Apple Vision), Tilely uses Apple's privacy-preserving system photo picker. Tilely does not request access to your full Photo Library and receives only the single photo you manually choose. All image processing happens 100% on-device.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Tilely Pro (Monthly, Annual with optional 7-day free trial, and Lifetime one-time purchase) is processed exclusively by Apple via the App Store and StoreKit 2. We never see, collect, or store your name, Apple Account, payment card, or billing details. Payment processing, Family Sharing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Contacting us

If you email us at support@melyro.app, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use this information solely to respond to your support request and fix reported issues, and we delete support correspondence upon request. Under KVKK Article 9(6), any cross-border email transfer occurs strictly incidentally upon your direct inquiry.

7. Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K & KVKK)

Tilely is designed for parents and children building together. We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children or adults. There are no ads, no social chat, and no external marketing links. Purchase initiation inside the app is gated by a lightweight adult Parental Gate to help prevent accidental purchases by young children.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK (Article 11 & Article 13), and U.S. state privacy laws (including CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold—which applies only to support emails you chose to send us, because the app itself sends us no data. Write to support@melyro.app and we will respond free of charge within 30 days. Under KVKK, if you are not satisfied with our response you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board (kvkk.gov.tr).

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever add optional features that change how data is handled (such as optional iCloud sync), we will update this page and publish the new effective date before those changes take effect.
